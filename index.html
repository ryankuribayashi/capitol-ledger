Two Trackers

What it watches

Both trackers pull from the same idea: certain people have to disclose their stock trades by law, and the gap between when they trade and when the public finds out is itself informative.

Congressional Disclosures

Capitol Ledger

Tracks senators' and House members' stock trades filed under the STOCK Act, measuring disclosure lag against the 45-day legal limit, breaking trades down by filer, party, and ticker, and separately tracking the President's OGE Form 278/278-T trading disclosures (30-day window).

Legal limit45 days to file
RefreshDaily, 7:00 AM MT

Corporate Insider Trading

Insider Ledger

Tracks SEC Form 4 filings — executives, directors, and 10%+ owners buying or selling their own company's stock — and flags genuine multi-insider cluster buys (the highest-conviction signal) separately from single-insider accumulation or routine option exercises.

Filing window2 business days
RefreshDaily, 7:10 AM MT

First Backtest

Does copying these filings actually beat the market?

Run Sep 13, 2026 against a live Alpaca paper-trading connection. Method: for every disclosed congressional buy, simulate buying on the filing date — the earliest a real copy-trader could have acted — and hold to the present, then compare that return to SPY over the identical window.

Read this before drawing a conclusion: that average return is driven by a handful of big winners (ORCL +14.4%, EL +20.6%, META +12.4%) — on a trade-by-trade basis it's close to a coin flip whether any single copied buy beats just holding the index. Holding windows are also short and uneven (11–37 days). This is one data point from a naive "copy everyone" strategy, not a validated edge. The next pass filters trades to a member's own committee oversight jurisdiction — where research on congressional trading suggests any real signal actually lives.

How It Runs

Unattended, every morning

Both dashboards are maintained by scheduled research agents rather than manual updates — the pipeline below repeats daily without anyone opening a terminal.

01
Pull filings
Research new STOCK Act and SEC Form 4 filings from public sources each morning.
02
Verify & classify
Cross-check every figure before it's added; flag genuine multi-insider clusters vs. single-filer activity.
03
Update the ledger
Recompute disclosure-lag stats and trade tables; publish the refreshed dashboard.
04
Backtest
Run simulated trades through a connected Alpaca paper account to test the strategy against SPY.
